# Salifort Motors HR Analytics Project

## Project Overview
This project analyzes HR data from Salifort Motors to understand and predict employee turnover. The HR department wanted to understand what factors contribute to employee departures and develop data-driven strategies to improve employee retention.

## Business Problem
Salifort Motors' HR department collected employee data but needed help understanding what factors lead to employee turnover. Employee turnover is costly due to recruitment, training, and lost productivity, making retention a key priority.

## Dataset
The analysis used a dataset containing 14,999 employee records with various features including:
- Satisfaction level
- Last evaluation score
- Number of projects
- Average monthly hours
- Time at company (tenure)
- Work accidents
- Promotions in last 5 years
- Department
- Salary level

## Key Findings
1. Workload Impact:
   - Employees working on 6-7 projects showed high turnover rates
   - Those working significantly more than average (>167 hrs/month) were more likely to leave
   - All employees with 7 projects left the company

2. Satisfaction Patterns:
   - Employees who left had lower average satisfaction (0.44) vs those who stayed (0.67)
   - Low satisfaction was particularly noted in employees working excessive hours
   - Four-year tenured employees showed notably low satisfaction levels

3. Work-Life Balance:
   - Many employees worked well over standard hours (166.7 hrs/month)
   - High performers working long hours without promotion were likely to leave
   - The combination of overwork and lack of career growth was a key turnover driver

## Recommendations
1. Project Management:
   - Limit employees to maximum of 5 concurrent projects
   - Monitor and balance workload distribution

2. Work Hours:
   - Implement policies to prevent excessive overtime
   - Target a standard 167 hours/month workload
   - Monitor employees regularly exceeding standard hours

3. Career Development:
   - Create clear promotion pathways
   - Increase promotion opportunities for high performers
   - Regular career development discussions

4. Satisfaction Monitoring:
   - Regular satisfaction surveys
   - Special attention to 4-year tenure milestone
   - Exit interviews to gather additional insights
